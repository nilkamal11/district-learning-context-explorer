Architecture

One controlled path from download to interpretation

The database is rebuilt atomically: a refresh writes a temporary .building.duckdb, runs quality checks, and replaces the prior database only after the checks succeed.

  1. 01Verify

    Names, sizes, headers, required columns, and SHA-256 fingerprints

  2. 02Stage

    Explicit CSV parsing, all-string intake, and controlled type conversion

  3. 03Model

    Stable district dimension, analytical marts, coverage, and audit tables

  4. 04Match

    Deterministic context-only peer selection with recorded relaxation

  5. 05Quantify

    Pool-specific errors, coverage gates, precision flags, and intervals

  6. 06Publish

    Compact derived bundle, offline charting, and browser-side interaction

    Similarity first; outcomes later

    An explicit feature allowlist makes circular matching mechanically harder: no achievement estimate can enter peer selection.

    1. Freeze context at 2024 and require the selected grade in the district span.
    2. Begin with the same grade-span bucket and dominant locale, enrollment within 4×, and poverty within 15 percentage points.
    3. Scale numeric differences on national 5th-to-95th percentile ranges; compare composition vectors with Hellinger distance.
    4. Select up to 15 same-state peers. Require at least 10 before that pool becomes the primary benchmark.
    5. Select 20 national analogs outside the target state, with no more than three from any state.
    6. If a stage must relax, expose the stage in the dashboard rather than hiding it.

    Uncertainty and interpretation

    The display can refuse to overstate a result

    Same-state peers

    Use within-state standard errors. “Higher” or “lower” language requires 10 reporting peers, at least 70% peer coverage, and a target estimate that is not low precision.

    National analogs

    Use NAEP-linking-adjusted standard errors and remain descriptive. Shared state-level linking uncertainty makes a simple cross-state significance label too strong.

    Debugging story

    The sample passed. Row 1.24 million did not.

    A quoted district name containing a comma exposed why production-scale validation matters.

    1. Autodetection looked healthy.The initial sample contained mostly unquoted district names.
    2. The full extraction failed.The first observed pattern appeared at physical line 1,236,002.
    3. The parser contract became explicit.Delimiter, quote, escape, and all-string ingestion were specified before controlled TRY_CAST typing.
    4. The lesson became a test.Validate the complete path, not merely a convenient sample.
