What do ELA and mathematics measure?

Both results come from the Illinois Assessment of Readiness (IAR), the state assessment of grade-level Illinois Learning Standards in grades 3–8.

ELA · English language arts/literacy

Measures how students read and understand grade-level texts, use evidence, and communicate through writing. The dashboard shows the school percentage classified as proficient or above proficient.

Math · Mathematics

Measures grade-level mathematical knowledge, reasoning, and application. Students solve problems, explain mathematical thinking, and apply concepts in multi-step and real-world situations.

Proficiency rate
The percentage of tested students whose scale scores meet Illinois's proficiency benchmark. This dashboard uses Levels 4+5 for 2022–2024 and Levels 3+4 for 2025. Illinois changed the cut scores, so the break from 2024 must be interpreted cautiously.
Growth percentile
Compares a student's scale-score progress with Illinois academic peers who had similar prior scores. Values run from 1 to 99; 50 is expected growth. The dashboard reports the published school mean, not an individual score or percentage.
